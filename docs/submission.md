Title: CrossLedger: Consensus-screened native batch exchange

Description:

CrossLedger is a two-sided GenLayer call market over native units and quote credits. Leader and validators independently fetch hash-pinned lot descriptions, decide every criterion and audit source clauses. Exact all-PASS agreement admits raw inventory; FAIL blocks it and UNKNOWN requires review. Signed asks escrow units; bids reserve quantity times limit credits. After all seals, clearing maximizes volume, minimizes imbalance and settles at one price with price/time priority, refunding unused reserves atomically. Holders can transfer purchased units; participants may veto before clearing. The repo includes 70 direct tests, 1,296 exhaustive books, 48 generated books and seven transport tests. Three StudioNet markets demonstrate partial fills, noncrossing refunds, blocked/uncertain lots, a holder transfer and two expected rejections. All 29 finalized MAJORITY_AGREE receipts bind signers/calldata to full live state. Synthetic endowments are not GEN, cash or verified delivery.
